## PROMPT 51 — "Back to the Gym"

```
The SAME wide empty gymnasium interior as Scene 01 — same rows of treadmills,
ellipticals, air bikes in one-point perspective, same fluorescent light bars,
same mirror wall — but the light has changed: warm gold sunlight floods through
the far windows, washing the machines in gold and cream, the fluorescent bars
now dim cool accents. A single charcoal figure in a hoodie walks IN through the
far doorway, toward the machines, a thin gold wedge of light at his feet. One
treadmill display glows green — the same accent as Scene 01. Machines modeled
identically to Scene 01 (deep charcoal body, lighter ink side plane, cream
highlight seam, oval contact shadows) but warmer: gold seams instead of cream.
Palette: 70% cream-gold floor, 20% charcoal machines, 10% gold sunlight + one
green display. Left third empty with warm reflections. Light: the sun is the
key light now — everything warm, the cool fluorescents barely visible. No
letters, no numbers, no words, no logos, no faces. Mood: the same room, the
same machines — but this time someone is walking in, not out.
```

## PROMPT 52 — "$133 on Black" (black beat)

```
Pure black field. On the right third of the frame, a tall stack of flat gold
coins — eleven coins perfectly stacked, modeled with darker gold rims, lighter
gold faces, and thin cream crescent sheens. A single cream spotlight cone falls
from the top-left corner, a flat translucent wedge, landing on the stack. The
stack casts a long, thin black shadow to the right. Below the stack, a small
gold line — a thin horizontal stroke, no letters — serves as the value marker.
Palette: 85% black, 10% gold stack, 5% cream spotlight. Type will sit center-
left on black. Light: the spotlight is the only source; the stack's top coin is
the brightest object in the frame. No letters, no numbers, no words, no faces.
Mood: one number, in gold, on black — the whole story reduced to a stack of
coins.
```

## PROMPT 53 — "Scan the Statement"

```
An extremely long cream receipt snakes diagonally across cream paper, from
bottom-left to top-right, its length curling and folding slightly, filling the
frame. Dense rows of tiny ink line-items run along it (abstract strokes, no
numbers). A single charcoal mouse cursor arrow — thin, sharp, angled — points
at the receipt's middle rows. A gold highlight band (flat translucent gold
rectangle) sweeps the two rows just ahead of the cursor. The receipt is modeled
with a darker cream underside at the curls and a thin cream seam along its top
edge. Flat shadows fall from the folds. Palette: 70% cream, 25% charcoal, 5%
gold band. Left third: open cream desk with a faint ruled margin. Light:
top-left key, the gold band is the brightest zone. No letters, no numbers, no
words, no faces, no hands. Mood: the statement, end to end — and the cursor
keeps moving, row by row.
```

## PROMPT 54 — "Final Line" (black beat)

```
Pure black field. One thin horizontal gold line runs across the exact center,
from left edge to right edge, its right end capped with a single small gold dot.
Nothing else in the frame — no floor line, no vignette, no grain. The line has
a barely visible darker gold bottom edge for dimensionality; the dot is a
perfect circle with a darker gold rim. Palette: 97% black, 3% gold. Type will
sit center-left on black. Light: self-lit, faint glow — the dot is the
brightest point in the frame. No letters, no numbers, no words, no objects.
Mood: the end of the story — a single line, still running, with nowhere left
to hide.
```

## BASE STYLE LOCK — append to every prompt

```
Vector illustration, editorial documentary style, 1920x1080. Dimensional flat
shading: every object has 2-3 flat tone steps (body, side plane, highlight seam)
and a consistent 45-degree flat cast shadow — no gradients, no textures, no 3D.
Realistic photographic light logic applied to flat shapes: clear key light
direction, warm rim lights, cool fill, the brightest zone of the frame holds the
story. Palette locked: 70% warm cream paper, 20% deep charcoal ink, 10% gold
accent (+ one green accent max). Left third of every frame is empty for type.
Thick crisp ink outlines. No letters, no numbers, no words, no labels, no logos,
no faces, no hands.
```
