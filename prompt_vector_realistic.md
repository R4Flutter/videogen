# 5 Realistic-Vector Prompts — Scenes 01–05

Each prompt is engineered to produce a **premium editorial vector** (Vox / Half As Interesting / MagnatesMedia style): dimensional flat shapes, cinematic light, physical depth cues — not flat clip-art. Append the **BASE STYLE LOCK** at the bottom of each prompt.

## PROMPT 01 — "The Empty Gym" (HOOK)

```
Cinematic wide interior of a massive commercial gym at 6 AM, seen from low eye level
looking down the full length of the floor. Endless parallel rows of modern cardio
machines — treadmills, ellipticals, cross-trainers, air bikes — receding toward the
back wall in strict one-point perspective, every machine identical, spaced like
parked cars. Fluorescent ceiling light bars run the entire length of the room,
casting soft pools of cool light on the polished floor; a mirror wall on the right
reflects the empty rows. The floor is vast, bare, waxed — no people, no mats, no
weights. One single distant silhouette of a man in a hoodie walking OUT through the
far exit, a thin slice of morning light cutting through the doorway. The left third
of the frame is empty floor with a soft reflection of the light bars.

Vector illustration, editorial documentary style, dimensional flat shading:
each machine has a dark charcoal body, lighter ink side planes, a thin cream
highlight seam along the top edge, and a soft oval contact shadow beneath it.
Two-tone modeling, no gradients. Palette: 70% warm cream paper background,
20% deep charcoal ink, 10% one green accent on a single treadmill display glowing
in the distance. Cool-to-warm light contrast between the fluorescent bars (cream)
and the doorway light (gold). Thick crisp ink outlines, flat cast shadows at a
consistent 45-degree angle. No letters, no numbers, no words, no logos, no faces,
no hands. Photorealistic lighting logic applied to flat shapes: light comes from
above, shadows fall toward bottom-right. Mood: vast, sterile, expensive emptiness.
```

## PROMPT 02 — "The Arithmetic" (THE NUMBERS DON'T WORK)

```
Macro editorial composition on a plain cream paper desk surface: a single enormous
charcoal fountain pen nib hovers at a 40-degree angle above a long cream receipt
stretched horizontally across the frame, the pen mid-stroke drawing a long flat
cost line with a thin trail of wet ink. Beneath the line, a small stack of flat
gold coins stops halfway — the visible gap between where the coins end and where
the line continues is the entire point of the image. A thin gold dashed connector
arc spans that gap, drawing the eye across it. The receipt has dense rows of tiny
ink line-items (pure abstract strokes, no text), and the two rows nearest the pen
nib are swept with a soft cream highlight. The desk surface catches a warm edge
light from the left; the pen nib has a two-tone charcoal body with a single white
specular seam down its barrel.

Vector illustration, editorial documentary style, dimensional flat shading:
pen nib modeled with 3 tone steps (deep ink body, mid charcoal plane, cream specular
line), coins as layered circles with a darker gold rim, lighter gold face, and a
thin white crescent sheen on the upper-left of each. Flat 45-degree cast shadows.
Palette: 70% warm cream, 20% charcoal ink, 10% gold coins + one green accent coin
at the base of the stack. Left third of frame is empty cream with a faint ruled
margin line. No letters, no numbers, no words, no logos, no faces, no hands.
Photographic light logic: key light top-left, coins catching a rim light, the gap
between coin stack and line end is the brightest zone in the frame. Mood:
meticulous, forensic, quietly damning.
```

## PROMPT 03 — "The Gap" (THE ROOM TO CHARGE)

```
Two flat door frames standing side by side on a cream interior wall, seen
straight-on at eye level, a few inches of wall between them. LEFT FRAME: small and
short — 4% of the wall height, plain, thin, unremarkable. RIGHT FRAME: tall and
wide — 10% of the wall height, grand, with a slightly heavier casing. Between
them, a charcoal measuring arrow spans the vertical difference, its arrowheads
sharp, its shaft with a thin tick line at the top of the small frame and a thin
tick line at the top of the tall frame, emphasizing exactly how much taller the
right frame is. A faint warm light spills from inside the tall frame as a flat
gold wedge on the floor beneath it; the small frame is closed. Both frames cast
short flat shadows on the wall to the bottom-right.

Vector illustration, editorial documentary style, dimensional flat shading:
door frames modeled with two tone steps (deep ink jamb, mid charcoal face), a thin
cream highlight along the inner edge of the tall frame, floor shadows as flat
strips. The measuring arrow is pure charcoal with a gold tick on the tall-frame
end. Palette: 70% warm cream wall, 20% charcoal ink, 10% gold wedge light + one
green accent on the small frame's doorknob. Left third of frame is bare wall.
No letters, no numbers, no words, no logos, no faces, no hands. Photographic light
logic: even diffuse daylight from the left, the gold spill from the tall doorway
is the only warm light in the frame. Mood: the difference is obvious, and it's
the whole story.
```

## PROMPT 04 — "4% vs 10%" (THE LITTLE NUMBERS)

```
Two identical dense grids of one hundred simple charcoal stick figures each,
standing in perfect 10-by-10 formation on a cream floor, seen from a slightly
raised three-quarter angle. LEFT GRID: exactly four figures tinted flat gold —
one in each quadrant of the grid, no pattern to their placement. RIGHT GRID:
exactly ten figures tinted flat gold — scattered the same random way. A single
thin ink dividing line runs down the center of the frame between the two grids.
The figures are minimal rounded silhouettes — head circle, torso, legs — all
identical, no faces, no arms raised, standing still like tokens. The gold figures
catch a faint cream highlight on their shoulders; the charcoal figures are matte.
Behind each grid, a soft flat cast shadow pool on the floor.

Vector illustration, editorial documentary style, dimensional flat shading:
figures modeled with two tone steps (side plane slightly lighter, facing plane
darker) to give the grids depth at a glance, floor shadows as flat ovals at the
same 45-degree angle. The gold tint is a single warm gold across all fourteen
highlighted figures — identical, no variation. Palette: 70% warm cream floor and
background, 20% charcoal ink figures, 10% gold figures only. Left third of frame
is empty cream floor with a faint vanishing line. No letters, no numbers, no
words, no labels, no logos, no faces. Photographic light logic: flat even light
from above, the gold figures are the only saturated color in the frame, they pull
the eye instantly. Mood: the count is the message — four versus ten, and ten wins.
```

## PROMPT 05 — "It's the Product" (EXHIBIT A)

```
A single modern treadmill displayed like an art object on a round cream museum
pedestal in the center of a vast empty gallery, seen at eye level from a
three-quarter angle. The treadmill is the hero: dark charcoal body, cream running
belt, a thin green LED strip along its console. A soft overhead spotlight cone
falls from the top edge of the frame onto the treadmill — a flat translucent
cream-to-gold wedge of light. A velvet rope barrier (two thin charcoal posts and
a single sagging rope) stands a foot in front of the pedestal. Beside the
pedestal, a small placard stand with a blank cream plaque — intentionally empty,
no letters. The gallery floor is polished cream with a faint reflection of the
treadmill's shadow; the walls are deep charcoal, so the object floats in a pool
of light. Left third of the frame is open gallery floor catching the edge of the
spotlight.

Vector illustration, editorial documentary style, dimensional flat shading:
treadmill modeled with three tone steps (deep ink shadow side, charcoal body,
cream belt with a white specular seam), the spotlight as a flat geometric cone
with slightly transparent cream fill, the velvet rope with a thin gold clip.
Flat 45-degree cast shadows. Palette: 70% warm cream (floor, light, belt), 20%
deep charcoal (walls, treadmill), 10% gold spotlight + green accent LED. No
letters, no numbers, no words, no logos, no faces, no hands. Photographic light
logic: the gallery is dark, the treadmill is the only lit object, the spotlight
falls from directly above at a slight tilt. Mood: museum quiet, the object is
absurdly, perfectly preserved.
```

direction, warm rim lights, cool fill, the brightest zone of the frame holds the
story. Palette locked: 70% warm cream paper, 20% deep charcoal ink, 10% gold
accent (+ one green accent max). Left third of every frame is empty for type.
Thick crisp ink outlines. No letters, no numbers, no words, no labels, no logos,
no faces, no hands.
