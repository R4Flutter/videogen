## PROMPT 06 — "Bank Statement"

```
A long cream paper receipt unfurling diagonally across the frame from top-left to
bottom-right, its bottom edge curled like ribbon. Dense rows of tiny abstract ink
line-items run along it; three consecutive rows are swept with a thick gold
marker band. A large charcoal pen nib rests at the gold band, mid-notation. The
receipt is modeled in two tones: cream face and a slightly darker cream underside
visible at the curl. A flat cast shadow of the curl falls on the cream paper
beneath. The gold band is the only saturated element. Palette: 70% cream, 20%
charcoal ink, 10% gold band. Left third of the frame is empty cream desk with a
faint ruled margin line. Light: soft top-left key, the gold band is the brightest
zone in the frame. No letters, no numbers, no words, no logos, no faces, no
hands. Mood: an audit, page by page — one line changes everything.
```

## PROMPT 07 — "$86 → $219"

```
Two columns of flat gold coins on a cream floor, seen straight-on at waist height.
LEFT: a short column of eleven coins. RIGHT: a tall column of twenty-eight coins,
more than double the height, both columns perfectly stacked, slight overhang on
the taller column to show weight. A thin ink price tag leans against each column,
tall enough to match its height. A long ink arrow arcs from the left tag to the
right tag. Coins modeled with darker gold rims, lighter gold faces, thin white
crescent sheen upper-left on each. Flat 45-degree shadows from both columns. One
green accent coin in the left stack's base. Palette: 70% cream, 20% charcoal ink
(tags, arrow), 10% gold + one green. Left third empty floor. Key light top-left;
the shadow gap between the two columns is wide and empty — the emptiness sells
the difference. No letters, no numbers, no words, no faces. Mood: the price of
loyalty, doubled.
```

## PROMPT 08 — "Free-Trial Checkout"

```
A giant cream checkout card tilted 3 degrees on a cream background, seen
straight-on. On the card: one enormous flat green button shape occupying the
lower half of the card, and far below it, at the card's bottom corner, a tiny thin
charcoal "dismiss" link shape — barely a hairline with a faint underline. A
charcoal mouse cursor arrow hovers above the tiny link. The green button casts a
long flat shadow across the card. Card has a thin ink border and a small corner
fold. Dimensional shading: the button has a darker green bottom edge (2-tone),
the card has a slightly darker cream edge. Gold accent: a thin gold line at the
card's top edge. Palette: 70% cream (background + card), 20% charcoal ink, 10%
green button + gold line. Left third empty cream. Light: soft top light, the tiny
link is in the dimmest zone — almost invisible, by design. No letters, no
numbers, no words, no logos, no faces. Mood: one giant yes, one microscopic no.
```

## PROMPT 09 — "Cost Structure"

```
A long flat charcoal cost line runs horizontally across the lower third of cream
paper, from edge to edge. Above it rises a staircase of three flat gold blocks —
each step higher than the last, the tallest block on the right. A thin dashed
ink connector drops from each block down to the cost line. The gold blocks are
modeled with a darker gold side plane and a cream highlight seam on the top edge;
each casts a short flat shadow. Palette: 70% cream, 20% charcoal ink, 10% gold
blocks. Left third empty cream with a faint baseline. Light: top-left key, the
blocks' top seams catch the light. No letters, no numbers, no words, no faces.
Mood: a machine quietly ascending — every step costs someone something.
```

## PROMPT 10 — "Cost-to-Serve Spike"

```
A long flat charcoal line runs horizontally across cream paper; at the right
third it shoots almost vertically upward like a thin tower, twice the frame
height in implied scale, capped by a flat top. The spike has a two-tone model
(darker right plane catching a thin cream highlight on the left edge) and casts
a long flat shadow to the right. At the base of the spike, a single tiny gold
coin is about to roll under it. The coin has a gold rim, lighter face, white
crescent sheen. Palette: 70% cream, 20% charcoal, 10% gold coin. Left third empty
cream. Light: top-left key; the coin is the only warm object, its smallness
contrasts the spike's mass. No letters, no numbers, no words, no faces. Mood:
one customer walking into a wall — the wall wins.
```

## BASE STYLE LOCK — append to every prompt

```
Vector illustration, editorial documentary style, 1920x1080. Dimensional flat
shading: every object has 2-3 flat tone steps (body, side plane, highlight seam)
and a consistent 45-degree flat cast shadow — no gradients, no textures, no 3D.
Realistic photographic light logic applied to flat shapes: clear key light
direction, warm rim lights, cool fill, the brightest zone of the frame holds the
story. Palette locked: 70% warm cream paper, 20% deep charcoal ink, 10% gold
accent (+ one green accent max). Left third of every frame is empty for type.
Thick crisp ink outlines. No letters, no numbers, no words, no labels, no logos,
no faces, no hands.
```
