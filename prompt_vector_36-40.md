## PROMPT 36 — "Prime Signup"

```
A giant flat yellow button shape (rounded rectangle) on cream paper, straight-on,
filling the center of the frame. Thick charcoal ink outline; a long flat shadow
falls from its bottom edge. A single charcoal mouse cursor arrow hovers above it,
pointing down at the button. Far below the button, at the frame's bottom edge, a
tiny thin charcoal link line — a hairline with a faint underline, obviously
secondary. The button is modeled in two tones: yellow face, darker gold bottom
edge. The tiny link sits in the dimmest zone. Palette: 70% cream, 20% charcoal
(outline, cursor, link), 10% yellow button. Left third empty cream. Light: soft
top key, the button is the brightest object in the frame; the link is nearly
invisible. No letters, no numbers, no words, no logos, no faces. Mood: one
giant yes, one microscopic no — same as the checkout, bigger.
```

## PROMPT 37 — "Dark Pattern"

```
A cream wall with two doors, seen straight-on at eye level. LEFT: an enormous
green door, wide open, light spilling out as a flat cream-gold wedge across the
floor, its frame filling half the wall. RIGHT: a tiny gray door at the wall's
base, closed, barely a third of the big door's height, in the corner. A thin
charcoal arrow arcs from the big door's top edge down to the small door. The big
door is modeled in two tones (brighter green front, darker green edge); the
small door has a faint cream seam. Flat shadows from both. Palette: 70% cream
wall, 20% charcoal (arrow, small door), 10% green big door + gold wedge. Left
third bare wall. Light: the spill from the big door is the brightest zone; the
small door is in shadow. No letters, no numbers, no words, no faces. Mood: the
obvious way in, and the real way in — the arrow shows you which one you will
take.
```

## PROMPT 38 — "The Iliad"

```
An enormous open cream book on cream paper, seen straight-on, its two pages
fanned open like wings, filling 70% of the frame. The pages are modeled in two
tones (cream face, darker cream edge) with thin ink text lines as abstract
strokes. A single gold bookmark ribbon hangs straight down the center, over the
gutter, catching a thin cream seam. At the book's base, a tiny charcoal figure
silhouette stands for scale — smaller than one page. The book casts a large flat
shadow on the floor. Palette: 70% cream, 25% charcoal (text strokes, figure),
5% gold ribbon. Left third: open page with faint strokes (type zone overlaps
naturally). Light: top-left key, the ribbon is the brightest zone. No letters,
no numbers, no words, no logos, no faces. Mood: ancient, heavy, opened for the
first time — a book that has waited centuries.
```

## PROMPT 39 — "Homer's Worn Pages"

```
A thick closed book on cream paper, tilted 4 degrees, seen at eye level. The
fore-edge (page block) is visibly frayed and uneven — small irregular steps of
cream and charcoal-gray layers, worn by decades of handling. A worn leather
spine band wraps the left edge, darker charcoal with a thin gold foil line. The
covers are modeled in two tones (charcoal top cover, darker charcoal side). The
frayed page block catches a thin cream seam across its top. Flat shadow beneath
the book. Palette: 70% cream, 25% charcoal, 5% gold foil line. Left third empty
cream. Light: top-left key, the frayed block's seam is the brightest zone. No
letters, no numbers, no words, no logos, no faces. Mood: a book read until it
is falling apart — the paper remembers.
```

## PROMPT 40 — "FTC Release"

```
A stack of cream official papers on cream paper, seen straight-on at a slight
angle: the top sheet is lifted at one corner, revealing the sheets beneath
(thin cream layers). On the top sheet, a single large gold embossed seal circle —
a plain ring with a scalloped inner edge, no letters — sits centered above dense
ink text lines (abstract strokes). A charcoal fountain pen rests diagonally
across the stack, its nib near the seal. The stack is modeled with darker cream
edges and a flat shadow to the right. The seal has a darker gold rim and a thin
cream highlight. Palette: 70% cream, 25% charcoal, 5% gold seal. Left third
empty cream. Light: top-left key, the seal is the brightest zone. No letters,
no numbers, no words, no logos, no faces. Mood: official, final, stamped — the
government has weighed in.
```

## BASE STYLE LOCK — append to every prompt

```
Vector illustration, editorial documentary style, 1920x1080. Dimensional flat
shading: every object has 2-3 flat tone steps (body, side plane, highlight seam)
and a consistent 45-degree flat cast shadow — no gradients, no textures, no 3D.
Realistic photographic light logic applied to flat shapes: clear key light
direction, warm rim lights, cool fill, the brightest zone of the frame holds the
story. Palette locked: 70% warm cream paper, 20% deep charcoal ink, 10% gold
accent (+ one green accent max). Left third of every frame is empty for type.
Thick crisp ink outlines. No letters, no numbers, no words, no labels, no logos,
no faces, no hands.
```
