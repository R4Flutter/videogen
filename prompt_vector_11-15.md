## PROMPT 11 — "Ideal Customer"

```
A heavy cream gym door propped wide open in a deep charcoal hallway, seen
straight-on at eye level. Light spills from inside as a flat gold wedge across
the floor and lower wall. Just inside the doorway, a single charcoal gym
membership card lies on the floor — a thin rectangle with a faint notch, no
writing. The rest of the frame is ink darkness; the doorway is the only lit
zone. The gold spill is modeled in two tones (brighter gold near the door,
cream-gold further out). The card casts a small flat shadow. Green accent: the
card's top corner, a tiny green chip. Palette: 30% cream (spill + card zone), 60%
charcoal (hallway darkness), 10% gold spill + green chip. Left third is dark
wall — type will sit on ink. Light: everything comes from the doorway; the card
is the brightest object in the frame. No letters, no numbers, no words, no faces,
no hands. Mood: the door opens, the light is welcoming — that is the trap.
```

## PROMPT 12 — "Google Trends"

```
Four horizontal rows of flat charcoal blocks on cream paper, stacked vertically
with equal gaps — each row a different "year" (implied, no text). Every row
follows the same shape: a short climbing slope of blocks on the left, then a long
flat plateau of identical blocks. The rows grow progressively longer left to
right; the final row's plateau is the longest of all. A thin gold underline runs
beneath the final plateau, slightly longer than it. Blocks modeled with a lighter
top seam. Flat short shadows at 45 degrees. Palette: 70% cream, 25% charcoal, 5%
gold underline. Left third empty cream with faint axis hints (two thin baseline
ticks). Light: even top-left, the gold underline is the only warm mark. No
letters, no numbers, no words, no labels. Mood: the same spike every single year
— then silence.
```

## PROMPT 13 — "BREAKAGE" (black beat)

```
Pure black field, no floor line, no vignette. From the top-right corner, a single
thick cream paper shard breaks away and falls diagonally toward center-left,
trailing three small black fragments. A thin gold crack line runs along the
break's edge, jagged, like snapped paper. The shard is modeled in two tones:
cream face with a slightly darker cream edge catching a faint gold rim light on
its top edge. The falling fragments are outlined in thin cream. Palette: 90%
black, 8% cream, 2% gold. Type will live center-left on black. Light: a single
distant cool source, only the shard's edge catches it. No letters, no numbers,
no words, no faces. Mood: something breaks — and it is the middle of the film.
```

## PROMPT 14 — "Gift Card → Subscription"

```
A single flat gift card stands upright on cream paper, slightly tilted, with a
thin ribbon bow on its top edge and a small folded envelope behind it. From the
card's base, a long train of identical thin gold slivers marches off to the
right like dominoes — each sliver identical in height and width, perfectly
spaced, thirty in a row, the last ones receding smaller to imply distance. The
card is charcoal ink with a cream face and a thin green ribbon. Each gold sliver
has a darker gold edge seam. A flat 45-degree shadow from the card. Palette:
60% cream, 25% charcoal, 15% gold slivers (the slivers dominate — that is the
point). Left third empty cream. Light: top-left key, the slivers catch a thin
cream seam along their top edges. No letters, no numbers, no words, no faces.
Mood: one small gift, then an endless identical row — forever.
```

## PROMPT 15 — "Calendar of Charges"

```
A cream wall calendar with a simple ink grid (four rows of seven cells), hanging
straight on a cream wall, seen straight-on. In EVERY row, the same single day
cell is marked with a thick gold outline and a small gold dot inside it — the
four gold cells form a clean vertical column. A charcoal pointing hand silhouette
(no detail, no nails) hovers at the edge of the column. The calendar has a thin
ink border and a small hanging hole at top center. The gold column cells are the
only saturated elements; the rest of the grid is faint thin ink. A flat short
shadow falls from the calendar's bottom edge. Palette: 75% cream, 20% ink, 5%
gold. Left third bare wall. Light: even diffuse, the gold column is the brightest
zone. No letters, no numbers, no words, no logos, no faces. Mood: the same day,
every single month — marked in gold.
```

## BASE STYLE LOCK — append to every prompt

```
Vector illustration, editorial documentary style, 1920x1080. Dimensional flat
shading: every object has 2-3 flat tone steps (body, side plane, highlight seam)
and a consistent 45-degree flat cast shadow — no gradients, no textures, no 3D.
Realistic photographic light logic applied to flat shapes: clear key light
direction, warm rim lights, cool fill, the brightest zone of the frame holds the
story. Palette locked: 70% warm cream paper, 20% deep charcoal ink, 10% gold
accent (+ one green accent max). Left third of every frame is empty for type.
Thick crisp ink outlines. No letters, no numbers, no words, no labels, no logos,
no faces, no hands.
```
