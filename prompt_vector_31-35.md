## PROMPT 31 — "Streaming Launches"

```
A neat 4-by-2 grid of eight identical flat cream tiles on cream paper, straight-
on, evenly spaced. Each tile contains a single simple geometric glyph — circle,
square, triangle, arc, star, diamond, hexagon, wave — drawn in thin charcoal
outline, NO letters, no logos, no brand marks. One tile at center-left is filled
solid gold instead, its glyph in cream. Tiles modeled with a slightly darker
cream bottom edge; the gold tile has a darker gold underside. Flat short shadows
at 45 degrees. A thin gold underline runs beneath the grid. Palette: 70% cream,
20% charcoal glyphs, 10% gold tile. Left third empty cream. Light: even top-left,
the gold tile is the brightest zone. No letters, no numbers, no words, no faces.
Mood: a shelf of eight identical boxes — one of them glows.
```

## PROMPT 32 — "Household Stack"

```
A vertical stack of eight flat cream tiles on cream paper, perfectly aligned,
tilting 2 degrees to the left, seen straight-on. Each tile is a slightly
different height; the top tile is the smallest (newest). The stack is modeled
with a darker cream edge on the right side of each tile and a thin cream seam
between tiles. A thick gold underline runs beneath the bottom tile, slightly
wider than the stack. A flat shadow falls from the stack's base to the right.
Palette: 70% cream, 25% charcoal (edges, seams), 5% gold underline. Left third
empty cream. Light: top-left key, the seams catch thin highlights. No letters,
no numbers, no words, no logos, no faces. Mood: the stack grows one tile at a
time — and it is already taller than the viewer.
```

## PROMPT 33 — "Two Bills, One Total"

```
Two cream paper bills stand upright side by side on cream paper, leaning inward
slightly toward each other. Between them, a third, taller combined bill stands
straight, with a thick gold seal circle (plain ring, no letters) on its upper
face. The side bills are identical, modeled with darker cream edges; the middle
bill is slightly wider and modeled in two tones (front face lighter, side edge
darker). Flat 45-degree shadows from all three. Palette: 70% cream, 25% charcoal
(edges), 5% gold seal. Left third empty cream. Light: top-left key, the gold
seal is the brightest zone. No letters, no numbers, no words, no faces. Mood:
two small papers lean on one big one — the sum is the headline.
```

## PROMPT 34 — "Streamflation"

```
A short staircase of four flat gold steps rising left to right on cream paper,
each step lower, wider, and darker gold (top step lightest). On each step, a
small cream price tag shape hangs from a hairline pole — plain tag with a hole,
no numbers. The top step's tag is slightly larger and outlined in charcoal ink
instead of hanging free. A charcoal hand silhouette (no fingers detail, no
nails) places the top tag, thumb and forefinger holding the tag edge. Steps
cast short flat shadows; tags catch a thin cream seam. Palette: 70% cream, 20%
gold steps, 10% charcoal (tags, hand). Left third empty cream. Light: top-left
key, the top step is the brightest. No letters, no numbers, no words, no faces.
Mood: each step up costs a little more — the hand keeps climbing.
```

## PROMPT 35 — "$/mo Everywhere"

```
Four small flat charcoal silhouettes arranged in a loose arc on cream paper: a
car (profile), a printer (box with paper tray), a doorbell (rounded rectangle
on a small wall plate), a thermostat (circle with dial tick). Each has one
identical small gold badge pinned to it — same size, same shape (plain circle
with a tiny hook), no letters. The car is slightly larger than the others. All
four cast flat 45-degree shadows. The badges are the only saturated elements,
modeled with a darker gold edge. Palette: 70% cream, 25% charcoal, 5% gold
badges. Left third empty cream. Light: even top-left, badges catch a thin
cream seam. No letters, no numbers, no words, no faces. Mood: everything in
the house now wears the same small gold tag.
```

## BASE STYLE LOCK — append to every prompt

```
Vector illustration, editorial documentary style, 1920x1080. Dimensional flat
shading: every object has 2-3 flat tone steps (body, side plane, highlight seam)
and a consistent 45-degree flat cast shadow — no gradients, no textures, no 3D.
Realistic photographic light logic applied to flat shapes: clear key light
direction, warm rim lights, cool fill, the brightest zone of the frame holds the
story. Palette locked: 70% warm cream paper, 20% deep charcoal ink, 10% gold
accent (+ one green accent max). Left third of every frame is empty for type.
Thick crisp ink outlines. No letters, no numbers, no words, no labels, no logos,
no faces, no hands.
```
