## PROMPT 46 — "Symmetry"

```
Three flat gold steps rising on the left half of cream paper, three flat gold
steps descending on the right half — mirrored perfectly around a thin charcoal
axis line running vertically down the exact center. Left staircase: each step
higher than the last (low, mid, high). Right staircase: each step lower than
the last, mirroring the heights exactly. Both staircases cast flat shadows
toward their outer edges. Steps modeled with darker gold undersides and cream
top seams. Palette: 70% cream, 20% gold steps, 10% charcoal (axis, shadows).
Left third: the left staircase's bottom step and open cream. Light: even
top-left, both staircases catch identical seams — the symmetry is perfect.
No letters, no numbers, no words, no faces. Mood: what goes up, comes down —
the same number of steps, the same amount of money, reversed.
```

## PROMPT 47 — "July 8 2025"

```
A cream wall calendar on cream paper, straight-on, hanging slightly off-center
right: a simple ink grid of four rows and seven cells, faint thin lines. One
single day cell near the middle of the grid is ringed by a thick gold circle —
the only saturated element in the frame. A charcoal pen (thin cylinder, nib
visible) rests diagonally on the calendar's edge near the ringed cell. The
calendar has a thin ink border and a small hanging hole; the ringed cell is
modeled with a slightly brighter cream face. A flat short shadow falls from
the calendar's bottom edge. Palette: 75% cream, 20% ink, 5% gold ring. Left
third bare wall. Light: even diffuse, the gold ring is the brightest zone.
No letters, no numbers, no words, no logos, no faces. Mood: one day on a
calendar — circled months in advance, the day everything changes.
```

## PROMPT 48 — "The Opinion"

```
A cream legal document page on cream paper, straight-on, filling the center:
dense thin ink text lines running top to bottom (abstract strokes), with a
small charcoal seal circle near the bottom (plain ring, no letters). One
paragraph in the middle of the page is swept with a thick gold marker band —
a flat translucent gold rectangle over the text lines, slightly rotated, with
a thin ink underline beneath it. The rest of the page is untouched. The page
is modeled with a darker cream edge and a flat shadow behind it. Palette:
70% cream, 25% ink, 5% gold band. Left third empty cream. Light: top-left key,
the gold band is the brightest zone. No letters, no numbers, no words, no
faces. Mood: hundreds of paragraphs, one highlighted — the whole case is in
those lines.
```

## PROMPT 49 — "PROCEDURE" (black beat)

```
Pure black field. A single cream paper rectangle floats in the center, filling
about 30% of the frame, cracking and dissolving — its edges breaking into small
falling paper shards. The shards nearest the rectangle are cream; the shards
lower down turn gold as they fall. Thin jagged gold crack lines spread across
the rectangle's face. The rectangle is modeled in two tones (cream face, darker
cream edge). Shards are flat triangles and rectangles with thin cream outlines.
Palette: 85% black, 10% cream, 5% gold. Type will sit center-left on black.
Light: a single cool source from above, only the rectangle's top edge catches
it. No letters, no numbers, no words, no faces. Mood: the procedure begins —
the paper that protected them is coming apart.
```

## PROMPT 50 — "2026 — No Rule" (black beat)

```
Pure black field. One thin horizontal gold line runs across the exact center,
from left edge to right edge, its right end capped with a small gold dot. A
second gold stroke slashes across it at a 45-degree angle — a diagonal slash,
slightly longer than the line's thickness, positioned at center. Both strokes
have a barely darker gold edge for dimensionality. Nothing else: no floor, no
vignette, no grain. Palette: 97% black, 3% gold. Type will occupy the center-
left on black. Light: self-lit, faint glow. No letters, no numbers, no words,
no objects. Mood: the rule is gone — and the line stays, still charging.
```

```
Vector illustration, editorial documentary style, 1920x1080. Dimensional flat
shading: every object has 2-3 flat tone steps (body, side plane, highlight seam)
and a consistent 45-degree flat cast shadow — no gradients, no textures, no 3D.
Realistic photographic light logic applied to flat shapes: clear key light
direction, warm rim lights, cool fill, the brightest zone of the frame holds the
story. Palette locked: 70% warm cream paper, 20% deep charcoal ink, 10% gold
accent (+ one green accent max). Left third of every frame is empty for type.
```
