## PROMPT 01 — "The Empty Gym" (HOOK)

```
Cinematic wide interior of a massive commercial gym at 6 AM, low eye level looking
down the full length of the floor. Endless parallel rows of identical cardio
machines — treadmills, ellipticals, air bikes — receding in strict one-point
perspective like parked cars. Fluorescent ceiling light bars run the whole length,
casting soft pools of cool light on polished floor; a mirror wall on the right
reflects the empty rows. Floor vast, bare, waxed — no mats, no people, no weights.
One distant hooded silhouette walks OUT through the far exit, a thin slice of
morning gold cutting through the doorway. Left third empty with faint light-bar
reflections. Dimensional flat shading: each machine has deep charcoal body,
lighter ink side plane, thin cream highlight seam, soft oval contact shadow.
Two-tone modeling, no gradients. Palette: 70% cream paper, 20% deep charcoal,
10% one green accent on a single distant treadmill display glowing. Cool
fluorescent cream vs warm doorway gold. Thick ink outlines, flat 45-degree cast
shadows. Light from above, shadows toward bottom-right. No letters, no numbers,
no words, no logos, no faces. Mood: vast, sterile, expensive emptiness.
```

## PROMPT 02 — "The Arithmetic"

```
Macro editorial composition on a plain cream desk: one enormous charcoal fountain
pen nib hovers at 40 degrees above a long cream receipt stretched horizontally
across the frame, mid-stroke drawing a long flat cost line with a wet ink trail.
Beneath the line, a small stack of flat gold coins stops halfway — the visible gap
between coin stack and line end is the entire point. A thin gold dashed connector
arc spans the gap, dragging the eye across. The receipt has dense rows of tiny
abstract ink strokes (no text); the two rows nearest the nib are swept with a soft
cream highlight. Warm edge light from the left; nib modeled in three tones (deep
ink body, mid charcoal plane, cream specular seam). Coins: darker gold rim,
lighter gold face, thin white crescent sheen upper-left. Flat 45-degree shadows.
Palette: 70% cream, 20% charcoal ink, 10% gold + one green accent coin at the
stack base. Left third empty cream with a faint ruled margin. Key light top-left,
the gap between coins and line end is the brightest zone. No letters, no numbers,
no words, no logos, no faces. Mood: meticulous, forensic, quietly damning.
```

## PROMPT 03 — "The Gap"

```
Two flat door frames side by side on a cream interior wall, straight-on at eye
level, a few inches of wall between them. LEFT: small and short — 4% of wall
height, plain, thin, unremarkable, closed. RIGHT: tall and wide — 10% of wall
height, grand, heavier casing, a warm gold wedge of light spilling from inside
onto the floor beneath. A charcoal measuring arrow spans the vertical difference
between them, sharp arrowheads, tick lines at the top of each frame emphasizing
exactly how much taller the right frame is. Both frames cast short flat shadows
to bottom-right. Dimensional flat shading: door frames in two tones (deep ink
jamb, mid charcoal face), thin cream highlight on the tall frame's inner edge.
Palette: 70% cream wall, 20% charcoal, 10% gold spill + one green accent on the
small frame's doorknob. Left third is bare wall. Even diffuse daylight from the
left; the gold doorway spill is the only warm light. No letters, no numbers, no
words, no logos, no faces. Mood: the difference is obvious — and it is the whole
story.
```

## PROMPT 04 — "4% vs 10%"

```
Two identical dense grids of one hundred minimal charcoal stick figures each,
standing in perfect 10-by-10 formation on a cream floor, seen from a slightly
raised three-quarter angle. LEFT GRID: exactly four figures tinted flat gold, one
per quadrant, no pattern. RIGHT GRID: exactly ten figures tinted flat gold,
scattered randomly. A single thin ink dividing line runs down the center. Figures
are rounded minimal silhouettes — head circle, torso, legs — identical, no faces,
no raised arms, standing like tokens. Gold figures catch a faint cream shoulder
highlight; charcoal figures are matte. Soft flat oval shadow pools beneath each
grid. Two-tone modeling per figure (lighter side plane, darker facing plane) for
grid depth at a glance; gold tint identical across all fourteen figures. Palette:
70% cream floor/background, 20% charcoal ink, 10% gold figures only. Left third
empty cream with a faint vanishing line. Flat even top light; the gold figures
are the only saturated color and pull the eye instantly. No letters, no numbers,
no labels, no faces. Mood: the count is the message — four versus ten, ten wins.
```

## PROMPT 05 — "It's the Product"

```
A single modern treadmill displayed like an art object on a round cream museum
pedestal in the center of a vast empty gallery, three-quarter eye-level view.
Treadmill is the hero: dark charcoal body, cream running belt, thin green LED
strip on the console. A soft overhead spotlight cone falls from the top frame
edge — a flat translucent cream-to-gold wedge. A velvet rope barrier (two thin
charcoal posts, one sagging rope) stands a foot in front of the pedestal. Beside
it, a small placard stand with a blank cream plaque — intentionally empty. The
polished cream floor catches a faint reflection of the treadmill's shadow; walls
are deep charcoal so the object floats in a pool of light. Left third open floor
catching the spotlight's edge. Treadmill in three tone steps (deep ink shadow
side, charcoal body, cream belt with white specular seam); spotlight as a flat
geometric cone with slightly transparent cream fill; gold clip on the rope.
Palette: 70% cream (floor, light, belt), 20% deep charcoal (walls, treadmill),
10% gold spotlight + green LED accent. The gallery is dark, the treadmill is the
only lit object. No letters, no numbers, no words, no logos, no faces. Mood:
museum quiet — the object is absurdly, perfectly preserved.
```

## BASE STYLE LOCK — append to every prompt

```
Vector illustration, editorial documentary style, 1920x1080. Dimensional flat
shading: every object has 2-3 flat tone steps (body, side plane, highlight seam)
and a consistent 45-degree flat cast shadow — no gradients, no textures, no 3D.
Realistic photographic light logic applied to flat shapes: clear key light
direction, warm rim lights, cool fill, the brightest zone of the frame holds the
story. Palette locked: 70% warm cream paper, 20% deep charcoal ink, 10% gold
accent (+ one green accent max). Left third of every frame is empty for type.
Thick crisp ink outlines. No letters, no numbers, no words, no labels, no logos,
no faces, no hands.
```
