## PROMPT 26 — "Beat of Black"

```
Pure black field. One thin horizontal gold line runs across the exact center of
the frame, from left edge to right edge, slightly thicker at the left end
(tapering from 4px to 2px implied). Nothing else in the frame — no floor line,
no vignette, no grain. The line has a barely visible darker gold bottom edge for
dimensionality. Palette: 97% black, 3% gold. Type will occupy center-left on
black. Light: none — the line is self-lit, faintly glowing. No letters, no
numbers, no words, no objects. Mood: a held breath — the middle of the story,
silence before the reveal.
```

## PROMPT 27 — "Sawtooth → Exponential"

```
Left two-thirds of cream paper: a flat sawtooth ridge of charcoal blocks, six
identical teeth, quiet and repetitive. Right one-third: a single smooth gold
exponential curve rising steeply from the baseline and exiting the top-right
edge — a single fluid stroke, thick at the base, thinner at the peak. A thin
ink arrow spans the gap between the last sawtooth and the curve's base. The
sawtooth has lighter top seams; the curve has a darker gold underside edge
(2-tone). Flat short shadows from the ridge. Palette: 70% cream, 20% charcoal,
10% gold curve. Left third empty cream. Light: top-left key, the curve is the
brightest zone. No letters, no numbers, no words, no faces. Mood: the pattern
breaks — suddenly, and forever.
```

## PROMPT 29 — "Boardroom Chart"

```
A dark boardroom interior: a deep charcoal wall with a large blank cream screen
mounted at center, seen straight-on at eye level. On the screen, a single thin
gold rising line crosses from bottom-left to top-right, one smooth stroke. Three
charcoal chair silhouettes (simple rounded backs, no people) sit in the
foreground at bottom, facing the screen, their shadows pooling on a dark floor.
A faint cool rim light traces the top of the screen. The chairs are two-tone
(darker seat, lighter back). Palette: 60% charcoal (room), 30% cream (screen),
10% gold line. Left third is dark wall — type sits on ink. Light: the screen is
the only lit object; the room falls away into darkness. No letters, no numbers,
no words, no faces. Mood: a chart that went up — and the room that let it.
```

## PROMPT 30 — "Cable → Netflix"

```
A giant cream paper envelope stands upright on cream paper, torn open at the top
corner, modeled in two tones (cream face, darker cream flap). From the tear, a
single thin black cable spills out and coils loosely on the floor. At the
envelope's base, a small flat cream TV screen (thin rectangle, small stand)
shows a single gold play triangle — a plain geometric triangle, no letters. The
cable is matte black with a thin cream highlight seam along its top curve. Flat
45-degree shadows from envelope and TV. Palette: 70% cream, 25% charcoal (cable,
TV frame), 5% gold triangle. Left third empty cream. Light: top-left key, the
gold triangle is the brightest zone. No letters, no numbers, no words, no logos,
no faces. Mood: the old thing unwound, the new thing tiny — and already
winning.
```
