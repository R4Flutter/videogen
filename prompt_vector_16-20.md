## PROMPT 16 — "Bally Contract"

```
An extra-tall cream contract sheet stands upright like a monolith on cream paper,
seen straight-on, its top edge slightly above frame. Dozens of thin ink text
lines run down it (abstract strokes, no words). Near the bottom: one large empty
ink signature line. Beneath it: a dense block of tiny gray fine-print lines
compressed together — visually a solid gray band of micro-strokes, obviously
unreadable. The sheet is modeled with a darker cream edge on the right side and
a thin gold seam down the left edge. A flat cast shadow falls behind it to the
right. Green accent: a small green dot beside the signature line. Palette: 70%
cream, 25% ink, 5% gold seam + green dot. Left third empty cream. Light: top-left
key, the fine-print band is the dimmest zone. No letters, no numbers, no words,
no logos, no faces. Mood: one page that owns you — the fine print is the fine
print's point.
```

## PROMPT 17 — "Clippings / Complaints"

```
Three overlapping cream newspaper clippings scattered like a hand-held fan on
cream paper: one horizontal on top, one vertical behind-left, one angled
behind-right. Each clipping has dense ink text blocks and one large headline
block with NO letters (abstract gray bars). The top clipping is rotated 2
degrees straight and slightly larger. A gold paperclip holds the stack at the
top corner. Clippings modeled with darker cream edges and flat short shadows at
45 degrees; the top clipping's headline bar is a lighter gray to pop. Palette:
70% cream, 25% ink, 5% gold clip. Left third empty cream. Light: even diffuse,
the top clipping is brightest. No letters, no numbers, no words, no logos, no
faces. Mood: the paper trail — page after page of the same complaint.
```

## PROMPT 18 — "Wrong Tool"

```
A heavy charcoal sledgehammer laid flat on cream paper, head to the left, handle
running diagonally toward bottom-right. Beside the hammer head, a small gold
jigsaw puzzle piece that clearly does not fit — its tabs face the wrong way. A
thin gold question-mark shape (abstract curve with dot, no letters) floats above
the puzzle piece. The hammer is modeled in three tones: deep ink head, mid
charcoal handle, thin cream specular seam along the handle's top. The puzzle
piece has a darker gold edge. Flat 45-degree shadows. Palette: 70% cream, 25%
charcoal, 5% gold piece + mark. Left third empty cream. Light: top-left key, the
puzzle piece is the brightest object. No letters, no numbers, no words, no faces.
Mood: the right problem, the wrong instrument — by design.
```

## PROMPT 19 — "Planet Fitness Storefront"

```
A flat storefront facade on cream paper, straight-on: one wide window on the
left, a door on the right, a scalloped striped awning above both — stripes in
desaturated purple and warm gold (kept muted so the facade reads under ink type).
The window glows warm cream from inside; the door is ajar with a thin gold wedge
spilling onto the sidewalk. A single charcoal bench sits on the sidewalk left of
the door. The facade is modeled in two tones (darker cream side wall, brighter
front), the awning casts a flat striped shadow on the window. Palette: 65% cream,
20% charcoal, 10% muted purple + 5% gold wedge. Left third empty sidewalk. Light:
even daylight, the glowing window is the brightest zone. No letters, no numbers,
no words, no logos, no faces. Mood: cheerful on the outside — cheap, bright,
unthreatening.
```

## PROMPT 20 — "$10 Among Noise"

```
A wide sea of identical flat charcoal coins scattered loosely across cream paper,
overlapping slightly, dozens of them, receding smaller toward the edges. Among
them, near center-right, one single green coin — same size, same orientation —
surrounded by a small gap of empty paper so it reads as the hero. The green coin
casts a tiny gold glow (a soft flat gold ring beneath it, not a gradient). The
charcoal coins are matte with a slightly lighter top seam. The green coin has a
darker green edge and a cream crescent sheen. Palette: 70% cream, 25% charcoal,
5% green + gold ring. Left third is empty cream — the sea stops at the frame
edge. Light: even flat top light; the green coin is the only saturated object.
No letters, no numbers, no words, no faces. Mood: one cheap thing buried in a
crowd — and the crowd is the business model.
```

## BASE STYLE LOCK — append to every prompt

```
Vector illustration, editorial documentary style, 1920x1080. Dimensional flat
shading: every object has 2-3 flat tone steps (body, side plane, highlight seam)
and a consistent 45-degree flat cast shadow — no gradients, no textures, no 3D.
Realistic photographic light logic applied to flat shapes: clear key light
direction, warm rim lights, cool fill, the brightest zone of the frame holds the
story. Palette locked: 70% warm cream paper, 20% deep charcoal ink, 10% gold
accent (+ one green accent max). Left third of every frame is empty for type.
Thick crisp ink outlines. No letters, no numbers, no words, no labels, no logos,
no faces, no hands.
```
