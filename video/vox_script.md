## IMAGE PLAN — WHERE THE PICTURES GO

**The rule.** Vox style is a paper page; photographs are *printed plates pasted onto it*, not backgrounds. Only the photo modules take images — `doodle`, `collage`, `footage`, `callout`. The drawn modules (`kinetic`, `stat`, `chart`, `trace`, `funnel`, `trust`, `icon`, `map`, `quote`, `compare`) draw themselves and must stay image-free — a photo behind kinetic type is the "slow-zoom slideshow" this engine exists to avoid.

**The plate clock.** 16 photo beats × 3 framings = **48 image slots**. Each beat holds 7–11 s, so its three plates swap every ~3 s — the EditorialStill engine staggers the framings (`seg = 1 / n`), so no plate ever holds longer than a plate's share of the beat. One new picture every ~3 seconds of photo runtime; drawn beats carry the ink between them.
