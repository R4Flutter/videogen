## PROMPT 41 — "$2.5B Split"

```
A single large flat gold block on cream paper, straight-on, filling the center
of the frame. A thick charcoal crack runs down its exact middle, jagged, and
the two halves separate slightly — a visible gap of cream paper between them.
Small cream shards fall between the halves, catching thin gold highlights. Two
thin charcoal hands (silhouettes, no finger detail, no nails) hold each half
from the sides, pulling. The block is modeled in three tones: gold front,
darker gold side planes at the split edges, cream seam along the top. The
falling shards are outlined in thin gold. Palette: 70% cream, 20% charcoal
(crack, hands), 10% gold block. Left third empty cream. Light: top-left key,
the split faces are the brightest gold. No letters, no numbers, no words, no
faces. Mood: a fortune snapped in two — and both halves still fit in one hand.
```

## PROMPT 42 — "Adobe Filing"

```
A cream manila folder standing open on cream paper, its back flap leaning up,
seen at a three-quarter angle. Inside: a thick stack of cream pages, densely
inked with abstract strokes, the top page slightly lifted. A charcoal clip
grips the folder's edge; a small gold tab peeks from the side. The folder is
modeled in two tones (cream front, darker cream interior shadow), the page
stack with thin layered edges. Flat shadow to the right. Palette: 70% cream,
25% charcoal, 5% gold tab. Left third empty cream. Light: top-left key, the
page stack's top edge catches the light. No letters, no numbers, no words, no
logos, no faces. Mood: another file, another stack — the second time the paper
moves.
```

## PROMPT 43 — "50% Fee"

```
Four identical flat charcoal staircase steps rising left to right on cream
paper, each step a square block, evenly spaced. The TOP step is solid gold,
modeled with a darker gold underside. A single thin gold coin falls from the
top step's edge, mid-air, angled — and below it, an open cream hand silhouette
(no finger detail, no nails) catches it. The coin has a gold rim, lighter face,
thin cream crescent sheen. Steps cast flat shadows; the falling coin casts a
tiny flat shadow on the air behind it (offset strip). Palette: 70% cream, 20%
charcoal, 10% gold (step + coin). Left third empty cream. Light: top-left key,
the gold step is the brightest zone. No letters, no numbers, no words, no
faces. Mood: half of everything walks up the steps and into one hand.
```

## PROMPT 44 — "$2.5B vs $75M"

```
One enormous flat gold block dominates the right half of cream paper, straight-
on, its top edge above frame, filling 80% of frame height. One tiny gold coin
sits at the bottom-left corner of the frame for scale — smaller than the
block's corner radius. A thin charcoal bracket (measuring line with ticks)
spans the block's width at its base, from left edge of block to right edge.
The block is modeled with a darker gold side plane and a cream seam along the
top edge; the coin has a darker gold rim. Flat shadow to the right. Palette:
70% cream, 20% charcoal (bracket), 10% gold. Left third empty cream — the
emptiness makes the scale. Light: top-left key, the seam is the brightest line.
No letters, no numbers, no words, no faces. Mood: the whole is a mountain; the
part is a coin you could lose in a pocket.
```

## PROMPT 45 — "Courthouse"

```
A flat courthouse facade on cream paper, straight-on, seen from street level:
a wide triangular pediment on top, four columns with simple capitals (plain
square tops, no scrolls), a wide short staircase of five steps at the base, and
a single gold door at the center between the columns, slightly open with a thin
gold wedge of light spilling out. The facade is modeled in two tones: cream
front, darker cream side planes on the columns' right edges. Each column casts a
thin flat shadow to the right. The pediment has a thin ink outline. Palette:
70% cream, 25% charcoal (outlines, shadows), 5% gold door + wedge. Left third:
empty cream sky above the pediment. Light: even daylight, the gold doorway is
the brightest zone. No letters, no numbers, no words, no logos, no faces.
Mood: institution, permanence — the building where the numbers go to die.
```

## BASE STYLE LOCK — append to every prompt

```
Vector illustration, editorial documentary style, 1920x1080. Dimensional flat
shading: every object has 2-3 flat tone steps (body, side plane, highlight seam)
and a consistent 45-degree flat cast shadow — no gradients, no textures, no 3D.
Realistic photographic light logic applied to flat shapes: clear key light
direction, warm rim lights, cool fill, the brightest zone of the frame holds the
story. Palette locked: 70% warm cream paper, 20% deep charcoal ink, 10% gold
accent (+ one green accent max). Left third of every frame is empty for type.
Thick crisp ink outlines. No letters, no numbers, no words, no labels, no logos,
no faces, no hands.
```
