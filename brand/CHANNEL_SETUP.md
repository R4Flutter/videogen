## 0. DO THIS FIRST (before any cosmetic change)

These three checks matter more than the banner. Do not skip them.

### 0.1 — Check whether you have an impressions problem or a click problem

YouTube Studio → Analytics → Content → open any existing Short.

| What you see | What it means | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Impressions = 0** | YouTube is not showing the video at all. This is technical, not creative. | Go to 0.2 and 0.3 |
| **Impressions normal, views ~0** | You are being shown and swiped past. | First frame + first 1 second |

Do this before anything else. If impressions are zero, no amount of rebranding fixes it.

### 0.2 — Check the "Made for Kids" setting

Settings → Channel → Advanced settings → audience.

Satisfying/slime/ASMR channels get auto-classified as **Made for Kids** extremely often. MFK strips personalization, comments, and most Shorts feed distribution — it is the single most common cause of literal zero views on this exact type of channel.

- Channel-level: set to **"No, set this channel as not made for kids."**
- Then go video-by-video: Content → each video → Audience → **"No, it's not made for kids."**

Finance content is genuinely not for kids, so this is accurate, not a workaround.

### 0.3 — Deal with the old ASMR videos

This is the highest-leverage action in the whole document. Leaving them up keeps telling YouTube's classifier that this is an ASMR channel, which is exactly the signal you are trying to erase.

**Set every existing satisfying/ASMR Short to Private** (not Unlisted — Private removes it from channel context most cleanly). You have no views to lose.

Content → select all → Edit → Visibility → Private.

## 5. COUNTRY & LANGUAGE

### ⚠️ Country of residence: set it to **India**. Leave it alone.

An earlier version of this document said to set this to United States. That was wrong — correcting it here.

**Country of residence is a legal and tax setting, not an audience-targeting setting.** It determines YouTube Partner Program eligibility, which tax rules apply to you, and it is expected to match your AdSense account and tax residency. Setting it to the US while you live in India and hold an Indian AdSense account creates a mismatch that can block YPP approval or hold your payouts. It buys you **zero** additional US reach.

- **Country of residence:** India ✅

### What actually signals a US audience

None of it is a country dropdown. It's these, in order of weight:

1. **The content itself** — USD, 401(k), Roth IRA, HYSA, FICO, W-2. This is 80% of it. YouTube reads your language, on-screen text, and topic vocabulary and matches you to viewers who watch that vocabulary.
2. **Video language: English (United States)** — Settings → Upload defaults → Video language. Set this per-upload too. This one *is* worth doing.
3. **English (US) subtitles** on every Short.
4. **Posting at US evening hours** — see §8.

Your audience follows your content, not your address. Plenty of India-based creators run US-audience finance channels; none of them do it by lying on the residence field.

### Avatar: use B, not A

Both are in the folder so you can compare, but the size checks (`_avatar_*_sizecheck.png`) show it clearly. At 48px — the size a viewer actually sees in the Shorts feed — variant A's orange ring collapses into the letterforms and turns to mush. Variant B (ink disc, paper letters) holds contrast all the way down. Pick contrast over cleverness at small sizes.

`#personalfinance` `#moneybasics` + one topic-specific.

### Posting time

Two windows work for a US audience. Anchor on Eastern — it's where the most viewers are.

| Window | Eastern | IST (you) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lunch scroll | 11 AM – 2 PM | **8:30 – 11:30 PM** | You're awake for this one |
| Evening scroll | 6 PM – 9 PM | 3:30 – 6:30 AM next day | Slightly stronger; use Schedule |

Best single slot across studies: **Wednesday ~3 PM ET** (12:30 AM IST).

IST is ET + 9:30 in US summer, **+10:30 in US winter** — the gap moves an hour when
American clocks change in November and March. Check it then.

**At 0 views, none of this matters much.** Pick the 8:30–11:30 PM IST window because
you can actually hit it, hold it fixed for 30 days, then read your own data:
Analytics → Audience → **"When your viewers are on YouTube."** That heatmap beats
every general guide — but it needs viewers before it can tell you anything.
