# Why You're At 0 Views — And The 30-Day Fix

Honest read of the situation, then a plan. The uncomfortable parts are near the top on purpose.

---

## PART 1 — THE DIAGNOSIS

### The rebrand is not your main problem

You asked for a rebrand and you're getting one. But a banner has never moved a channel from 0 to anything. Channel art matters at the *conversion* step — someone already watched, liked it, and clicked through to see who you are. You are failing three steps earlier, at distribution. So treat the assets as necessary hygiene and put your actual effort into Parts 2 and 3.

### Four things are probably wrong, in order of likelihood

**1. You may be labeled "Made for Kids."**
Satisfying / slime / marble-run / kinetic-sand content is the single most auto-flagged category on YouTube. MFK kills personalized recommendation, which is 90%+ of Shorts distribution. This produces *exactly* the symptom you describe — literal zero, not "few." Check this before you do anything else (`CHANNEL_SETUP.md` §0.2). If this is it, everything else in this document is secondary.

**2. Your channel is classified as ASMR, and you're about to feed it finance.**
YouTube's 2026 model groups viewers into narrow interest micro-clusters and rewards channel cohesion. Your channel's entire signal set — title, description, 30+ ASMR keywords, and every uploaded video — says "oddly satisfying." When you post a finance Short, the first test audience YouTube assembles will be ASMR viewers. They will swipe in under two seconds. That swipe rate is the metric that decides whether the video gets a second, larger test audience. It won't.

This is survivable but you have to actively clear the old signal, not just add a new one on top. Private the old videos. Wipe the keywords. Change name, handle, description, and art *in the same session*, not spread over weeks.

**3. Your hook is competing at the wrong resolution.**
The metric that governs Shorts is roughly "viewed vs. swiped away" — the share of people who don't flick past. Strong Shorts sit around 70–90%. Below ~60% and distribution stalls. Your `script_vox.md` opens with *"Your savings account pays 0.4%. Prices rose 3%. You lost money by saving it."* — that's a genuinely good hook, and the contradiction structure is right. But your first *frame* is text landing word-by-word on a still paper background. In a feed, a static beige frame with partial text reads as "nothing is happening" for the ~0.4s a thumb needs to decide. Fix: the full hook line should be **fully legible on frame one**, at maximum size, with the animation happening *after* comprehension, not before it.

**4. You are building for two different countries at once.**
`script_vox.md` is USD, US-centric, tier-1 targeted — correct. `story.txt` is ₹100/day, lakh, crore — an India-audience script. If you post both, you don't get two audiences. You get one incoherent cluster and YouTube stops being able to place you. Pick USA, as you said, and then be ruthless: no ₹, no lakh/crore, no SIP, no "Rs." Use 401(k), Roth IRA, HYSA, FICO, W-2, 1099. Archive `story.txt` or rewrite it in USD.

### The risk you asked me to name: should you keep this channel?

Keeping it is defensible **only because it has zero views.** There is no watch-history graph to poison, no subscriber base to alienate, no recommendation model deeply trained on you. A rebrand at 0 views is nearly as clean as a fresh start.

But be clear-eyed about what you're keeping: you are keeping a channel whose only asset is that it already exists. If you find you're at MFK-locked status, or if 3 weeks of daily consistent finance uploads still produce zero impressions, a fresh channel costs you nothing and removes every doubt. Set that as an explicit tripwire rather than something you agonize over later:

> **Tripwire: 21 consecutive daily uploads. If impressions are still ~0 across all of them, start a new channel and re-upload the best 10.**

The reason not to start fresh *today* is that a brand-new channel has its own cold-start problem, and you'd be trading a known issue for an unknown one. The reason to hold the tripwire is that sunk cost is the thing that actually kills channels like this.

## PART 2 — WHAT ACTUALLY MOVES THE NUMBER

Four changes, ordered by impact per hour spent.

### 2.1 — First frame carries the whole video

Rewrite the opening beat so the complete hook sentence is readable in frame one at maximum type size. Your `kinetic` module currently reveals word-by-word — keep that motion for *later* beats where it adds rhythm, but the hook should land whole and then get emphasized. A viewer decides before your second word arrives.

Your paper-and-ink identity is a real asset here, not a liability: in a feed of high-saturation neon finance content, a calm off-white frame with heavy black type is *pattern interrupt*. Lean into it. Just make sure the type is big enough to read at phone size and the first frame isn't half-empty.

### 2.2 — Say the number out loud in the first 2 seconds

Contradictions beat curiosity gaps. "0.4% is a loss" works. "Here's what nobody tells you about savings" doesn't. You already know this — your script does it. Keep doing it in every single script.

### 2.3 — Post daily, at a fixed US-evening slot, for 30 days

Not because volume is magic, but because YouTube needs repeated samples to reclassify your channel, and because Shorts distribution has become heavily recency-weighted — Shorts older than roughly 28–30 days get sharply fewer impressions. One video a week gives the classifier almost nothing to work with. Batch-render on weekends, schedule the week out.

### 2.4 — Kill the CTA in the first 40 seconds

"Subscribe" before you've delivered value is a swipe trigger. Payoff first, ask second, and keep the ask to one short line.

## PART 3 — THE 30-DAY PLAN

### Day 0 — The switch (do it all in one sitting, ~1 hour)

- [ ] Check impressions in Studio (0 vs. low — this tells you which problem you have)
- [ ] Set channel + all videos to **not** Made for Kids
- [ ] Private all existing ASMR/satisfying Shorts
- [ ] Change name → `Real Return`, handle → `@realreturn` (or first available)
- [ ] Replace description and **delete all 30+ ASMR keywords**, paste the finance set
- [ ] Upload banner, avatar (variant B), watermark
- [ ] Country → United States, upload default language → English (US)
- [ ] Archive or rewrite `story.txt` out of rupees

### Days 1–7 — Prove the pipeline

Ship one Short per day. Do not chase topics — use the seven most universal, most-searched money questions in the US, because they're the ones with the broadest test audience:

1. Why a savings account can lose money (you already have this one written)
2. What compound interest actually does to $200/month
3. HYSA vs. checking — the actual dollar difference in year one
4. What an expense ratio costs you over 30 years
5. 401(k) match = the only guaranteed 100% return you'll ever get
6. Why paying minimums on a credit card takes 18 years
7. Roth vs. Traditional in one sentence of arithmetic

Every one: one mechanism, one number, one payoff. All USD.

### Days 8–14 — Read the data, not your feelings

Open Studio → each Short → check **"Viewed vs. swiped away."**

- Any Short above 70% → make three more videos on that exact topic
- Any Short below 50% → the hook failed, not the topic. Rewrite the first 3 seconds and re-upload as a new video

Ignore views this week. VVSA is the leading indicator; views are the lagging one.

### Days 15–21 — Double down and hit the tripwire check

- Keep shipping daily; bias toward whatever cleared 70%
- Reply to every comment within an hour of posting
- **Day 21: run the tripwire.** Still ~0 impressions across 21 uploads? Start fresh, re-upload your best 10, keep this channel private as an archive.

### Days 22–30 — Consolidate

- Keep daily cadence
- Build a 3–5 Short series on your best-performing topic (series drive the "watch another" signal)
- Add one 3–5 minute long-form version of your single best Short — long-form is where finance actually monetizes, and it strengthens topical classification

---

## PART 4 — WHAT TO IGNORE

- **Subscriber count.** At this stage it's noise. VVSA and impressions are the only two numbers that mean anything.
- **Buying views or engagement.** Actively harmful — it teaches the classifier the wrong audience, which is the exact problem you're already digging out of.
- **Posting to 6 platforms at once.** Not until one is working.
- **Redesigning the visual system again.** It's good. The paper/ink/orange identity is more distinctive than 95% of finance Shorts. Stop touching it and go write scripts.
