# REAL RETURN — Brand Guide

The channel and the video engine share one identity. These tokens are the same ones in
`video/src/theme.ts` under `theme.vox` — change them there and here together, or not at all.

---

## Name

**Real Return** · `@realreturn`

**Tagline:** The money math nobody showed you.
**One-liner:** One idea, 60 seconds, no hype.

The name is the thesis. "Real return" is the actual financial term for what's left after
inflation — the number nobody quotes and everybody lives with. It doubles as a promise
about the channel: real, not hype.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `paper` | `#F4F1EA` | Background. Everything sits on this. |
| `paperDeep` | `#E4DED1` | Gradient falloff, vignette, second surface |
| `ink` | `#1A1A1A` | All primary type. Never pure black. |
| `accent` | `#D9491E` | **One** emphasis per frame. Circles, underlines, the one word that matters. |
| `muted` | `#8A857C` | Secondary type, captions, footers |
| `rule` | `#C9C2B4` | Hairlines, grid, dividers |

**The restraint is the look.** Do not add a sixth colour. Do not add green for "up." If a
frame needs two accents, the frame is doing two jobs — split it into two beats.

## Type

**Archivo** — Black for wordmarks and headlines, Bold for eyebrows, Semibold for taglines.

Loaded via `@remotion/google-fonts` at render time. The `brand/` PNGs were generated
offline in **Lato Black**, the closest available stand-in — if you ever regenerate them on
a machine with Archivo installed, swap the font path in `brandkit.py` and they'll match
the videos exactly.

| Role | Weight | Tracking | Case |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wordmark / headline | Black | tight (−2 to −4) | UPPER |
| Eyebrow / kicker | Bold, small | wide (+6 to +8) | UPPER |
| Tagline | Semibold | wide (+5) | UPPER |
| Caption / body | Bold | normal | Sentence |

## The marker

The hand-drawn orange ellipse and underline are the signature — the thing that makes a
frame recognizable as yours at a glance. Rules:

- **One marker per frame.** Two is a mess.
- It marks *the* word or *the* number, never a whole sentence.
- It should look drawn, not printed — wobble is the point.
- In video, it draws in over ~0.4s on the beat the word is spoken.

Generated by `roughjs` in the Remotion engine; by `rough_ellipse()` / `rough_underline()`
in `brandkit.py` for stills.

## Voice

**Is:** calm, mechanism-first, arithmetic on screen, second person, US-native.
**Is not:** hyped, urgent, guru-ish, emoji'd, "let me tell you a secret."

Every video is one subtraction, one comparison, or one compounding. If you can't state
the mechanism in a single sentence, it's two videos.

**Currency and vocabulary: USD only.** No ₹, lakh, crore, SIP, or "Rs." Use 401(k),
Roth IRA, HYSA, APY, FICO, W-2, expense ratio, emergency fund.

**Every number on screen must match the number spoken.** This is a trust channel; one
mismatch costs more than a bad hook.

## Beat vocabulary

The engine's Vox modules map to a fixed narrative shape. Keep the order.

| Beat | Module | Job |
|---|---|---|
| The Claim | `kinetic` | A contradiction the viewer can check on their own statement |
| The Status Quo | `footage` | What everyone believes, over archival |
| The Pivot | `doodle` | Name the real measurement, circle it |
| The Mechanism | `icon` | Three steps, one line icon each |
| The Escalation | `chart` | Run the same math forward or backward until it hurts |
| The Payoff | `kinetic` | Reframe the thing itself. No CTA before this. |
