## PROMPT 21 — "$10/mo vs $120/yr"

```
Split-screen composition on cream paper with a single thin ink dividing line down
the exact center. LEFT HALF: one single flat gold coin standing on edge, thin
profile view, with a small flat shadow. RIGHT HALF: a tall thin charcoal column
made of stacked identical coin slivers — twelve slivers of equal height, each
with a faint seam line, reaching 75% of frame height. A tiny green flag shape
(plain triangle on a hairline pole) stands atop the right column. Both halves
are empty cream above their objects. The column has a slightly lighter front
plane; the single coin has a darker gold rim. Palette: 70% cream, 20% charcoal,
10% gold coin + green flag. Left third empty (left half is already minimal).
Light: top-left key, both objects catch a thin top seam. No letters, no numbers,
no words, no faces. Mood: one vs twelve — the column looks small because the
single coin is the honest one.
```

## PROMPT 22 — "Boxed CS6"

```
A gold software box standing upright on cream paper, tilted 4 degrees, seen at
eye level. On its front: a dark charcoal panel with a plain geometric glyph
(abstract diamond, no letters). A flat white shrink-wrap sheen — a translucent
polygon wedge — crosses the box diagonally. At the box's base, a charcoal disc
(a CD) leans against it, catching a thin cream highlight. Beside the box, a thin
paper sleeve with a plain barcode stripe (abstract vertical bars of varying
widths, no numbers). The box is modeled in three tones: gold front, darker gold
side, thin cream seam along the top edge. Flat 45-degree shadow beneath. Palette:
70% cream, 20% charcoal (panel, disc, sleeve), 10% gold box. Left third empty
cream. Light: top-left key, the shrink-wrap sheen is a flat white zone, not a
gradient. No letters, no numbers, no words, no logos, no faces. Mood: a
beautiful box that costs more than the machine it runs on.
```

## PROMPT 23 — "Revenue Sawtooth"

```
A single towering sawtooth ridge made of flat charcoal blocks on cream paper,
seen straight-on: six identical teeth, each block a square of equal size, the
ridge filling the right 60% of the frame and rising 70% of frame height. The
ridge casts a long flat shadow to the right. One gold block is inserted at the
base of the last tooth — slightly offset, the only warm element. Blocks modeled
with a lighter top seam; the ridge has a slightly darker side plane where the
teeth overlap. Palette: 70% cream, 25% charcoal, 5% gold block. Left third empty
cream. Light: top-left key, each tooth catches a thin cream seam. No letters, no
numbers, no words, no faces. Mood: steady, identical, relentless — the same
tooth every quarter.
```

## PROMPT 24 — "Adobe MAX 2013"

spotlight is the brightest zone; the audience sits in dimmer cream. No letters,
no numbers, no words, no logos, no faces. Mood: one moment on a stage — the
audience already paid to be there.

## PROMPT 25 — "The Reaction"

```
Three vertical cream paper comment cards floating on cream paper at slight
rotations (-2, 0, +3 degrees), the middle card straight and largest. Each card
has dense ink text lines and a small charcoal avatar circle with NO face (plain
circle, no features). Beside the middle card, a gold upvote arrow (thin
triangle) floats, larger than the text lines. Cards modeled with darker cream
edges and flat short shadows. The middle card's text lines are slightly lighter
gray so the gold arrow dominates. Palette: 70% cream, 25% charcoal, 5% gold
arrow. Left third empty cream. Light: even diffuse, the middle card brightest.
No letters, no numbers, no words, no faces. Mood: the internet weighs in — one
arrow, a thousand voices.
```

## BASE STYLE LOCK — append to every prompt

```
Vector illustration, editorial documentary style, 1920x1080. Dimensional flat
shading: every object has 2-3 flat tone steps (body, side plane, highlight seam)
and a consistent 45-degree flat cast shadow — no gradients, no textures, no 3D.
Realistic photographic light logic applied to flat shapes: clear key light
direction, warm rim lights, cool fill, the brightest zone of the frame holds the
story. Palette locked: 70% warm cream paper, 20% deep charcoal ink, 10% gold
accent (+ one green accent max). Left third of every frame is empty for type.
Thick crisp ink outlines. No letters, no numbers, no words, no labels, no logos,
no faces, no hands.
```
